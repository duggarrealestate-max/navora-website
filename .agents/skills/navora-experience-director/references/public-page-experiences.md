# Public page experiences

These are direction for future authorized work, not a claim that the current pages already implement them. Each major page has one memorable interactive centrepiece; legal pages remain restrained and readable.

| Route | Centrepiece and purpose |
| --- | --- |
| `/` | Preserve the approved six-zone robot experience: ATTRACT, ENGAGE, CONVERT, DELIVER, RETAIN, GROW. Robot appears nowhere else. |
| `/solutions` | Interactive automation theatre showing TRIGGER → UNDERSTAND → DECIDE → ACT → HUMAN CONTROL → OUTCOME. Make a scenario, rule, approval or exception change the visible result. |
| `/industries` | Adaptive automation scanner covering repeatable processes across many business types. Never restrict NAVORA to four industries. Use adaptable scenarios and clearly illustrative examples rather than claiming unsupported coverage or clients. |
| `/how-it-works` | Interactive pathway showing UNDERSTAND → MAP → DESIGN → BUILD → CONNECT → LAUNCH → IMPROVE. Let the visitor inspect how information and decisions progress through the pathway. |
| `/about` | Abstract intelligence structure connecting people, processes and outcomes. Keep it honest and founder-led; use verified founder facts and no invented team, clients or results. |
| `/contact` | Project-initiation interface responding honestly as the enquiry form is completed. Distinguish local completeness, validation, sending, actual success and failure; do not show delivery or acceptance before the real adapter confirms it. |
| `/privacy`, `/terms` | Restrained technological motion. Readability, stable headings, legal content and navigation have priority. |
| Public unmatched route (404) | Lost-system-route experience with clear recovery actions to home, solutions or contact. Preserve usable HTML and avoid endless animated dead ends. |

Current redirect aliases are `/services` → `/solutions`, `/work` → `/industries`, `/process` → `/how-it-works`. Preserve them and inspect their destinations. Existing similarly named page files are not proof of active routes; read current routing before adding pages.

## Living-system states

NAVORA visibly demonstrates intelligent systems completing work. Choose states appropriate to the scenario:

1. Trigger received
2. Context understood
3. Rule checked
4. Decision selected
5. Action started
6. Approval required
7. Exception escalated
8. Customer updated
9. Connected system updated
10. Task completed
11. Outcome recorded

Approval and exceptions are conditional branches, not compulsory successful steps. Stop at human control until a real user choice permits progress. A replay or reset must restore a coherent state. Clearly label simulated automation; never perform external actions just to animate a public demo. Keep external capabilities behind repository interfaces.

Where appropriate, panels enter, receive information, change status, connect to another system, complete and then minimise or leave. They must not remain static. Show what changed and why, with textual status as well as motion. Provide useful loading, empty and error states wherever applicable, as required by `AGENTS.md`.

## New pages

Choose a centrepiece rooted in that page's real purpose, then fit it to the established visual system. Wire the authorized route into the public shell without changing internal applications. Add the route to the verification script's route inventory and to browser inspection coverage.
